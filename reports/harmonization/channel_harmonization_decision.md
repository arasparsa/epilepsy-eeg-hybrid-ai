The primary montage was selected to maximize recording,

case, seizure, and ictal-time retention while preserving

bilateral temporal, parasagittal, posterior, and midline

coverage.



The inconsistent T8-P8 derivation was excluded from the

primary analysis to avoid relying on unverified aliases.
